Potato in Oven

Potatos in Oven are great

Ingredients

  • Potatos
  • Cream
  • Cheese
  • Garlic
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Butter
  • Thyme

Steps

  1. Add cream , garlic , butter to a mixing cup
  2. Pour it into your sliced potatoes first layer
  3. Add salt , pepper, thyme
  4. You have done your first layer! do 3 layers total!
  5. Bake without cheese (cover it with alaminimum foil)
  6. Add cheese (Carefull hot!) and bake it for some more time
  7. Lets eat!
